Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who we are

LeadOrbit (“we”, “us”) provides an AI lead and marketing workspace for agencies and growth teams. This policy explains what data we process when you use LeadOrbit, including Meta (Facebook) ads features.

Data we collect

  • Account & org data — name, email, organization settings you provide.
  • Lead data — information submitted on landing pages (for example name, email, phone).
  • Meta connection data — ad account, Page, Pixel, and Instant Form identifiers you select; OAuth tokens stored to create and manage ads on your behalf.
  • Usage logs — basic technical logs needed to operate and secure the service.

How we use data

  • To run LeadOrbit features you request (Connect Meta, campaign launch, lead capture).
  • To create and manage Meta campaigns only on ad accounts you authorize.
  • To improve reliability, prevent abuse, and comply with law.

We do not sell personal data.

Meta / Facebook

When you connect Meta, LeadOrbit uses Facebook Login for Business and the Marketing API with permissions you approve. Tokens are stored securely for your organization and used only to provide Connect Meta and campaign features. You can disconnect Meta in LeadOrbit settings; you should also revoke access in your Facebook Business / App settings if needed.

Sharing

We use infrastructure providers (for example hosting and database) to run the product. They process data only to provide those services. We may disclose information if required by law.

Retention

We keep data while your organization uses LeadOrbit and for a reasonable period afterward for backups, disputes, or legal requirements, then delete or anonymize it where practical.

Your choices

  • Update or delete leads and connection settings inside LeadOrbit.
  • Disconnect Meta OAuth from Connect Meta.
  • Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal data we hold.
